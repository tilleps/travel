# Australia


- Do not tip, workers are paid a liveable wage, it's not expected, and the locals hate it.
- Wear sunscreen!  The sun in Australia is brutal even when cloudy.


## Sydney

Did you know "The Matrix" movie was filmed in various locations around Sydney?  The lady in red dress scene was filmed at [Lloyd Rees Fountain](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5ATAY8aWAMMqjVqo6)

In Australia, instead of "Downtowns", they have "CBDs" (City Business Districts).  The Sydney CBD is pretty much everything between Central Station and the Sydney Opera House.



### Transportation

Public transportation is pretty decent in Sydney, don't really need a car for most places.  You can use a credit card to pay.  Make sure to tap on and off when you get on and off the bus, train, and light rails.  Google maps works pretty well.  The Metro is the newest and fastest way to get around between suburbs.



### Food Recommendations

I found Chinese and Thai food in Sydney to be generally pretty good (I usually look for/order Pad Kra Pao).  Mexican food is pretty disappointing though.  The beef in Australia does taste a bit different, even in fast food chains like McDonald's and Hungry Jack's (Burger King).  The new [Sydney Fish Market](https://maps.app.goo.gl/macS486mJcGcezAa6) is a nice place to see and visit, but the food is just average.  If you do go, try to go earlier than later as most of the vendors are sold out / closed by afternoon.

If you get a chance, should try fries with chicken salt - very addicting.

### Things to See

- **[Sydney Opera House](https://maps.app.goo.gl/qoBpm8QEokq11QYH6)**: Iconic landmark, there's a bar underneath for typical tourist food and drinks.
- **[Zephyr Bar](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gatQJh8kRBtTYRvN7)**: Cocktail bar with a nice view of Darlington Harbor
- **[Bondi Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Tk49nx2Nf4JxTgn38)**: World famous beach, there's a nice hiking path along the shore on the south side of the beach.
- **[Manly Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5JtSScWVub9gevuNA)**: Popular and nice beach, lots of hiking paths and beaches in the surrounding area.  Take the ferry from Sydney Harbor (near the [Sydney Opera House](https://maps.app.goo.gl/qoBpm8QEokq11QYH6))
- **[Darlington Harbour Fireworks](https://maps.app.goo.gl/V2g83BMTk4vNCCeA8)**: Saturday Sept 5th at 8:30pm
- **[Darlington Harbour](https://maps.app.goo.gl/i7Q6Nhz9kYwFr5da6)**: I recommend walking around Darlington Harbour at night then walk north towards Bangaroo along the boardwalk.  Can walk to the Barangaroo Metro station to get back to the center of the city.
- **Wildlife**: Birds, koalas, kangaroos, and wombats that poo cubes.  They have zoos and wild life parks where you can get close and pet/hold them.  If you're lucky you can sometimes see koalas in trees.  The kangaroos are like Australia's version of a deer.
